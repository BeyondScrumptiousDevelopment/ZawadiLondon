Questions

How do I order?

All orders are placed through our Etsy shop.

Will I see a proof first?

We don’t send proofs for the frame, so please double-check names, dates and spelling when you order.

Can I ask for something custom?

Yes — send us a custom enquiry and tell us about your moment.
