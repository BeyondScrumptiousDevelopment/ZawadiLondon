Our story

Zawadi means “gift” in Swahili, and every frame we make is exactly that: a gift, shaped around one moment and the people in it.

Zawadi London transforms life’s special moments into beautifully crafted keepsakes — personalised, thoughtfully designed and made especially for you. A new arrival. A wedding day. A graduation. The photo you keep coming back to.

We take our time over every detail, so what arrives feels as meaningful as the moment itself.
